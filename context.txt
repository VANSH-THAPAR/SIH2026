PROJECT CONTEXT — SIF SENTINEL

We are building a solution for Smart India Hackathon 2026 Problem Statement SIH26165.

Problem Statement

Title:
AI/NLP Engine to Detect Serious Injury & Fatality (SIF) Precursors in OIL's Unsafe-Act/Unsafe-Condition and Near-Miss Reports

Organization: Oil India Limited (OIL)

Problem

OIL collects a large number of:

Unsafe Act (UA) observations
Unsafe Condition (UC) observations
Near-miss reports
Incident reports

These reports contain valuable safety information, but manual review/triage can delay identification of events that had Serious Injury or Fatality (SIF) potential.

The objective is to build an AI/NLP system that can analyze these unstructured reports and identify situations that could realistically have resulted in serious injury or fatality.

The required capabilities are:

Classify a report as SIF-potential or non-SIF-potential
Map it to the relevant IOGP Life-Saving Rule
Identify recurring precursor patterns, especially:
activity
location/site
barrier failure
Provide an interactive dashboard that helps HSE teams prioritize sites/activities with high SIF-precursor density.
OUR PRODUCT

Working name:

SIF Sentinel

Core positioning

An explainable AI-powered SIF early-warning system that transforms unstructured HSE observations into actionable safety intelligence by detecting SIF potential, identifying failed safety barriers, discovering recurring systemic patterns, prioritizing risk, and recommending targeted HSE interventions.

The core idea is:

Report → Understand → Detect → Diagnose → Discover → Prioritize → Act

We do not want to build a system that simply says:

“SIF = Yes/No.”

That is too basic.

Our differentiation is:

Don't just tell HSE that a report is dangerous. Tell them why it is dangerous, which safety barrier failed, whether the same failure is recurring elsewhere, how the risk is evolving, and what intervention should happen next.

IMPORTANT DATA ASSUMPTION

The real source of reports is the OIL HSE platform.

We are not claiming that users will manually upload reports into our production system.

The intended architecture is:

OIL HSE Platform
      ↓
UA/UC + Near-Miss + Incident Reports
      ↓
SIF Sentinel Intelligence Pipeline

For the hackathon prototype/demo, because we may not have a live OIL HSE API, we will simulate an incoming HSE report through our interface.

For example:

"During maintenance, a technician started opening
a pipeline valve before confirming that the line was
fully depressurized. No injury occurred."

This simulated report goes through the exact same pipeline that a real HSE report would use.

DATA STRATEGY

We may not have access to actual historical OIL HSE reports.

Therefore, we will build a domain-specific synthetic dataset.

We must NEVER claim that synthetic data is real OIL data.

We should say:

“Since historical OIL HSE reports are not publicly available to us, we developed a domain-specific synthetic dataset modeled around the safety scenarios and report structure relevant to the problem statement. The architecture is designed so the synthetic dataset can later be replaced by real OIL HSE data.”
