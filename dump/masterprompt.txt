==================================================
1. PROJECT
==================================================

Build:

AI-Powered SIF Precursor Detection & Risk Intelligence Software

The application converts OIL HSE/HSSE safety reports into structured safety intelligence and helps HSE users:

- understand what happened
- assess SIF potential
- identify failed critical barriers
- map Life-Saving Rules
- find similar historical incidents
- detect recurring/emerging patterns
- understand escalation pathways
- prioritize risk
- create interventions
- track actions
- evaluate intervention outcomes

Core flow:

HSE REPORT
    ↓
UNDERSTAND
"What happened?"
    ↓
ASSESS
"Could it realistically become fatal?"
    ↓
IDENTIFY
"Which critical barrier failed?"
    ↓
MAP
"Which Life-Saving Rule applies?"
    ↓
REMEMBER
"Has OIL seen this before?"
    ↓
DETECT
"Is a dangerous pattern emerging?"
    ↓
RECONSTRUCT
"How could it escalate?"
    ↓
PRIORITIZE
"What should HSE act on first?"
    ↓
INTERVENE
"What action should be taken?"
    ↓
MEASURE
"Did the intervention work?"

Central differentiator:

"We don't just detect SIF precursors; we explain the pathway from precursor → failed barrier → potential escalation → historical pattern → preventive intervention."
